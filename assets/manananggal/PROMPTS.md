# Manananggal (winged upper body): view prompts for ChatGPT

Generate all four in the **same ChatGPT chat**, in this order. Save them as `front.png`,
`right.png`, `left.png`, `back.png` in `assets/manananggal/reference/` and have each one checked
before spending Tripo credits (60 with texture).

Why she is posed this way: the wings point up and out while the arms point down and out, so in the
front view nothing overlaps and Tripo does not fuse arm to wing. The wings are fully open and flat
so they can be given bones and made to flap. The torn waist is short and chunky, because long thin
dangling strands come out as broken spikes in 3D. The eyes do not glow; the glow is added in the game.
Her lower half (the legs she leaves behind) is not in these pictures; it is made separately for free.

```text
Character reference for a 3D model, photorealistic, portrait orientation.

A manananggal from Philippine folklore: only the upper half of a woman's body, from the waist up, floating in the air with nothing below the waist. The body ends at the waist in a short, ragged, torn edge with a few thick, short, clumped strands of dark flesh hanging no more than a hand's length. No legs, no hips, no long dangling entrails.

She is gaunt, with ashen grey-brown skin stretched over ribs and collarbones, a sunken face, dull dark-red eyes with no glow, and a slightly open mouth with long sharp fangs. Long, matted black hair hanging straight down behind her back in one heavy mass, behind the shoulders, not over her face, chest, arms or wings. Long bony arms with long fingers ending in dark curved claws. She wears a filthy torn off-white baro blouse with short ragged sleeves that covers her chest and ends in tatters above the waist.

Two huge bat wings grow from her shoulder blades. Each wing has a thick bony arm, long finger-bones, and a dark leathery membrane with a deep red-violet tint and a few small tears. 

Pose: body upright and straight, facing the camera directly. Both wings fully open, flat and symmetrical, raised up and out in a wide V shape, about 30 degrees above horizontal, every finger-bone spread so the membranes are stretched tight. Both arms straight, held down and out from the body at about 40 degrees, well below the wings, so the arms never touch or overlap the wings. Hands empty, fingers straight and spread. Head level, looking straight ahead.

Camera at chest height, straight-on, no perspective distortion, like a long lens. The whole figure including both wing tips fully in frame, with empty space all around. Soft, flat, even studio lighting, no strong shadows, no rim light, no glow, no fog, no blood dripping. Plain light-grey seamless background. No text, no labels, no ground, no shadow below her.
```
