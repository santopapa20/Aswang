# Aswang (transformed): view prompts for ChatGPT

Generate all four in the **same ChatGPT chat**, in this order, so each one can copy the one before.
Save them as `front.png`, `right.png`, `left.png`, `back.png` in `assets/aswang/reference/` and have
each one checked before spending Tripo credits.

Why the design differs slightly from the concept sheet: she stands upright with arms out (the hunch
comes later from animation), her hair is kept behind her shoulders, the skirt stops at the knee, and
the eyes do not glow. Each of those keeps the 3D shape clean; the glow is added in the game.

## 1. Front

```text
Full-body character reference for a 3D model, photorealistic, portrait orientation.

A transformed aswang from Philippine folklore, 1950s rural Visayas: a gaunt, emaciated woman with ashen grey-brown corpse-like skin stretched over visible ribs, collarbones and joints. Sunken cheeks, a heavy brow, dull dark-red eyes with no glow, mouth slightly open showing rows of sharp yellowed teeth. Long, matted, wet-looking black hair hanging straight down her back in one heavy clumped mass, tucked behind her shoulders, not covering her face, chest or arms. Arms slightly longer than human, with long bony fingers ending in dark curved claws. Bare feet with long clawed toes. She wears a filthy, torn, off-white baro blouse with short ragged sleeves ending above the elbow, and a ragged dark patadyong skirt torn off unevenly at knee height, so both lower legs are fully visible and apart. Dried mud and old dark stains on the cloth.

Pose: standing fully upright, straight back, facing the camera directly, in a strict A-pose. Both arms straight and held out from the body at about 40 degrees, well clear of the torso and skirt. Hands empty, palms facing the thighs, all fingers straight, spread and clearly separated. Legs straight, feet shoulder-width apart. Head level, looking straight ahead.

Camera at chest height, straight-on, no perspective distortion, like a long lens. Whole figure in frame with space above the head and below the feet. Soft, flat, even studio lighting with no strong shadows, no rim light, no glow, no fog. Plain light-grey seamless background. No text, no labels, no props, no ground shadow.
```
